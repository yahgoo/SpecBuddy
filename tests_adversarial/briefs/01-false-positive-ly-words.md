# Supply Chain Management Requirements

## Functional Requirements

- WHEN a new order arrives, THE System SHALL notify the supply team within 30 seconds.
- THE System SHALL display the product family tree for each catalog item.
- WHEN assembly instructions are uploaded, THE System SHALL validate the PDF structure.
- THE System SHALL generate a monthly report summarizing all transactions.
- THE System SHALL allow users to apply discount codes at checkout.
- WHEN the user submits an early registration, THE System SHALL confirm enrollment within 5 seconds.
- THE System SHALL support the import of commodity data in a tab-delimited format only.
- WHEN inventory falls below the reorder threshold, THE System SHALL create a supply request.
- THE System SHALL display assembly status for each manufacturing lot.
- THE System SHALL restrict access to the admin panel to authorized personnel only.
- WHEN a flight is cancelled, THE System SHALL rebook the affected family members automatically.
- THE System SHALL archive completed orders on a quarterly basis.
- THE System SHALL accept uploads in the GeoTIFF format natively.
- WHEN the user selects a belly band style, THE System SHALL preview the wrapping layout.
- THE System SHALL enforce a mandatory cooling-off period of 14 days for yearly subscriptions.
